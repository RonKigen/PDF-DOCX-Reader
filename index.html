Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

PDF Reader - PDF Viewer ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

1. Information Collection & Local Processing

Our application is built on an offline-first architecture.

  • Local Documents: All PDF, Word (.docx), and image files stored, viewed, edited, or converted within the app remain strictly on your device's local storage. We do not upload your documents, notes, e-signatures, or scans to any remote server.
  • Camera Access: If you use the document scanner feature to take photos of physical papers or whiteboards, the images are processed locally on your device for OCR (text recognition) and PDF conversion. They are never transmitted or stored externally.

2. Permissions

The app may request certain permissions solely to deliver core functionality:

  • Storage / Manage External Storage: Required to auto-scan, list, read, and manage PDF and document files stored on your device.
  • Camera: Required only when you explicitly choose to scan physical documents or business cards into the app.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate external advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or cloud analytics tools that collect your personal data. Your usage data stays completely private.

4. Data Security

Because your files and documents are processed and stored locally on your device, you maintain total control over your data security. Protected files locked with passwords remain encrypted locally.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
